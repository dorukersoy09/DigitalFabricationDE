Connect the 12V battary to the l298n’s(the red board)

- red cable to the one that says 12V(+)
- black cable to the one that says GND(-)
- 5V can stay empty
- after, skrew them to place and make sure the metal parts touch

DC (yellow motor) motors two cables one to OUT1 the other to OUT2

![rasberypico.webp](R2-D2%20Robot/rasberypico.webp)

after to connect this to the pico connect

- **IN1 of** l298n to Pico's **GP14** pin.
- **IN2** **of** l298n to Pico's **GP15** pin.

![PCA9685.jpeg](R2-D2%20Robot/PCA9685.jpeg)

now to connect the two servo motors we connect

- Now connect the servo motor to the pingroup where it says ”0”
- Now connect the servo motor to the pingroup where it says ”1”

now to connect the PCA9685 board to pico

- **VCC of** PCA9685 to Pico's **3V3** pin.
- **GND** **of** PCA9685 to Pico's **GND** pin.
- **SDA** **of** PCA9685 to Pico's **GP0** pin.
- **SCL** **of** PCA9685 to Pico's**GP1** pin.

# Notion documentation

## how i created the Notion page

1. **Opened an account on Notion.** I signed up and gained access to my workspace.
2. **Created a new page.** I titled it *“Doruk’s DigiFab Website”* as my homepage.
3. **Used the “/” command.** This let me add headings, bullet lists, checklists, images, and other elements to organize my content.
4. **Wrote an introduction.** I added an “About Me” section with my name, grade, and interests.
5. **Customized the design.** I added a cover image, icon, and toggles to make the page look clean and engaging.
6. **Built sections and subpages.** I structured the site into clear categories:
- Project Management & Documentation (Notion)
- Vinyl Cutting & Vectorial Design (Inkscape, Cricut, GS2-24)
- 2D Design & Laser Cutting (Cuttle XYZ, Laser Cutting)
- 3D Design & Printing (Onshape, 3D Printing, Blender)
1. **Documented setup.** I included text and screenshots for each step.
